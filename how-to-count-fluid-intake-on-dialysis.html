How to Count Fluid Intake on Dialysis on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Renvi developer

Short answer. Enter the daily fluid limit your dialysis team gave you in Renvi, then tap Glass, Cup or Sip each time you drink. Drinks you add from the food list count too, and you can switch on counting the water inside food. Follow the limits your care team gave you; Renvi is not a medical device.

Step by step

  1. Pick your unitIn Settings choose ml or oz for fluid.
  2. Enter the fluid limitUnder Daily limits type the number your care team gave you. Renvi has no default.
  3. Tap as you drinkOn Today, the fluid card has Glass (240 ml or 8 oz), Cup (150 ml or 5 oz) and Sip (30 ml or 1 oz). Each tap adds one entry.
  4. Fix mistakesEvery entry sits in a row under the buttons. Tap the x on an entry to remove it.
  5. Decide about food waterTurn on Count the water in food as fluid in Settings if your team wants soups, fruit and similar foods included. Ask your team which way they want it counted.

Frequently asked questions

Do ice and soup count?

Ask your care team how they count them. Renvi lets you log them as drinks, and the food-water switch adds the water listed for each food.

How much water is in food?

USDA SR Legacy lists, per 100 g, about 95 g in raw cucumber, 91.5 g in raw watermelon and 94 g in prepared canned chicken noodle soup.

Can I change the size of a glass?

The three buttons are fixed sizes: 240, 150 and 30 ml (or 8, 5 and 1 oz). Tap more than once for larger amounts.

What is my fluid limit?

That is for your doctor or dialysis team to say. Renvi only compares your total with the number you type.

Does Renvi suit people on peritoneal dialysis too?

The onboarding asks about hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, neither, or caring for someone. The logging works the same; the limits come from your own team.

Is the day cut at midnight?

Entries are stored by calendar day on your phone.
