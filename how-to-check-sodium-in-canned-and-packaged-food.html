How to Compare Sodium in Canned and Packaged Foods on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Renvi developer

Short answer. Search the food in Renvi and open the entries for the regular and the low-sodium version, because the USDA database lists them separately. Add what you eat to a meal and the sodium bar on Today shows the running total. Follow the limits your care team gave you; Renvi is not a medical device.

Step by step

  1. Set your sodium limitEnter the number from your care team in Settings under Daily limits, or leave it empty to only count.
  2. Search by foodIn Foods, search for example pickles or cottage cheese. Several entries appear, including no salt added and reduced sodium versions.
  3. Compare entriesOpen each entry and read sodium for the same portion.
  4. Log your choiceAdd to a meal. The sodium bar moves.

Frequently asked questions

How different are regular and reduced-sodium versions?

USDA lists dill pickles at 809 mg sodium per 100 g and the reduced-sodium version at 18 mg. Cottage cheese 1% is 406 mg and the no-sodium-added entry is 13 mg.

Does soup differ a lot?

Per 100 g, USDA lists canned chicken noodle condensed at 681 mg, prepared with water at 335 mg, and the dry mix at 3161 mg.

What about bread?

White bread, commercially prepared, is listed at 490 mg per 100 g, and the low-sodium version at 298 mg.

What sodium limit should I use?

Use the one your care team gave you.

Can Renvi read the label on my can?

No. It uses database entries. If your product differs, Pro lets you add a food with the numbers on its label.

Is Renvi a medical device?

Renvi is not a medical device. Follow the limits your care team gave you and ask them before changing your diet.
