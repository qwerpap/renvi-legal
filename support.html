Support

Renvi: Kidney Diet Tracker · Last updated September 27, 2026

Something not working, a food missing, or an idea? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we answer within two working days. In the app, Settings › Write to us adds your app version automatically.

Common questions

  • Where do the numbers come from? USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy — about 6,900 foods, built in and available offline.
  • What limits should I use? The ones your doctor or dietitian gave you. Renvi never suggests limits. If you don't know them yet, leave them empty and Renvi just counts.
  • A food isn't in the list. Foods › Create — type the values from the label once.
  • Restore a purchase. Settings › Restore purchases, or Restore on the Pro screen.
  • Cancel a subscription. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Renvi.
