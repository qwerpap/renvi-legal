How to Track Potassium on a Kidney Diet on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Renvi developer

Short answer. Type the daily potassium limit your doctor or renal dietitian gave you into Renvi, then search each food, pick the portion and add it to a meal. The Today screen shows a potassium bar that fills with every entry. Follow the limits your care team gave you; Renvi is not a medical device and sets no limit of its own.

Step by step

  1. Enter your limitIn Settings, under Daily limits, type the potassium number your care team gave you. If you do not have one yet, leave it empty and Renvi only counts.
  2. Search the foodOpen Foods and type the name, for example banana or potato. Or browse by aisle.
  3. Choose the portionUse the portion control to set grams, or pick a household portion such as a cup or a slice. The potassium for that amount is shown, with the share of your limit if you set one.
  4. Add it to a mealTap Add to breakfast, lunch, dinner or snacks. The potassium bar on Today moves right away.
  5. Compare similar foodsWith Renvi Pro, the lower-potassium list shows foods of the same kind with less potassium, so you can compare before choosing.

Frequently asked questions

How much potassium is in a banana?

USDA SR Legacy lists raw banana at 358 mg potassium per 100 g, and the entry has portions such as a cup of slices (150 g) or mashed (225 g).

Why do potatoes matter so much for potassium?

USDA lists baked potato with skin at 535 mg per 100 g, and a large potato is 299 g, so one potato adds up quickly. Seeing the portion total is the point of logging.

Is a leafy green low in potassium?

Not always. USDA lists raw spinach at 558 mg per 100 g, but a cup of raw spinach weighs about 30 g, so the portion matters as much as the food.

What limit should I use?

Use the number your care team gave you. Renvi does not suggest one.

Do cooking and brands change the numbers?

Yes. Database values are typical for the food as described. Packaged foods can differ, and Renvi does not read labels.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the food data is stored in the app.
