Potassium, Phosphorus, Sodium

Renvi is a kidney diet tracker for people who were told to watch potassium, phosphorus, sodium, protein or fluid. Enter the limits your doctor or renal dietitian gave you, then log what you eat and drink — Renvi keeps a running total for the day, so you know how much room is left before dinner.

What Renvi does

  • Look up any food offlineAbout 6,800 entries from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) ship inside the app. No account, no connection needed.
  • Six numbers per foodPotassium, phosphorus, sodium, protein, calories and water, shown for the portion you pick, in cups, slices, pieces or grams.
  • Your limits, your numbersYou type the daily limits your doctor or renal dietitian gave you. Leave one empty and Renvi simply counts.
  • One bar per nutrientEach meal fills the bars on the Today screen, so you can see what is left before you plan dinner.
  • Fluid by the glassTap Glass, Cup or Sip as you drink. Choose ml or oz, and optionally add the water inside food.
  • Pro: history, charts and a PDFLook back over 7 or 30 days, send a one-page report to your dietitian, find lower-potassium swaps, and add foods of your own.

Frequently asked questions

What is Renvi?

Renvi is an iPhone food and drink diary for people who track potassium, phosphorus, sodium, protein or fluid. It adds up what you log and compares the totals with limits you enter yourself.

Does Renvi tell me how much potassium or fluid I should have?

No. Renvi has no built-in limits and does not pick a stage or a protocol. Follow the limits your care team gave you and type them into Settings.

Is Renvi a medical device?

Renvi is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, monitor or treat any condition and does not replace advice from your doctor or dietitian.

Where do the nutrient numbers come from?

From the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy dataset, which is public domain (CC0). Values are typical for the food as described and differ between brands and ways of cooking.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The food database is stored on the phone and your diary stays on the phone.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Food lookup, logging, daily limits and the Today screen are free. History, charts, the PDF report, swaps and custom foods need Renvi Pro.

Does Renvi scan photos or barcodes?

No. You search by name and choose a portion.
