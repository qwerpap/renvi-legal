How to Log Phosphorus in Food on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Renvi developer

Short answer. Search a food in Renvi, set the portion, and read the phosphorus for that amount before you add it to a meal. The Today screen keeps a running phosphorus total against the limit you entered. Follow the limits your care team gave you; Renvi is not a medical device.

Step by step

  1. Turn phosphorus onIn the setup quiz pick the nutrients you watch, or open Settings and type your phosphorus limit under Daily limits.
  2. Search the foodOpen Foods and search by name, for instance cheddar or lentils.
  3. Check the portionSet the amount in grams or choose a household portion. Phosphorus is shown in mg for that portion.
  4. Add it to a mealChoose Add to the meal. The phosphorus bar on Today updates.
  5. Save the foods you repeatFoods you eat often appear under Your usual foods, and Pro lets you add your own foods with your own numbers, for example from a label.

Frequently asked questions

How much phosphorus is in common foods?

Per 100 g, USDA SR Legacy lists cheddar cheese at 455 mg, cooked lentils at 180 mg, whole milk at 84 mg and cooked white rice at 43 mg.

Does Renvi know about phosphate additives?

No. The database covers the food as described by USDA. Additives in packaged foods may not be reflected, so check labels and ask your dietitian.

Why per portion, not per 100 g?

A cup of lentils is 198 g, so about 356 mg by USDA data. Renvi does that multiplication for the portion you choose.

What phosphorus limit should I use?

The one your care team gave you. Renvi does not provide one.

Can I add a packaged food that is not in the list?

With Renvi Pro you can add a food with your own numbers.

Is it accurate for my brand?

Values are typical and vary by brand and cooking.
